## The four targets and their boundaries

### R1 — Explicit K4 sampler

Source: [bounded-bit construction](notes/k4_bounded_bit_full_linear_sampler.md), SHA-256 `ab21002e44b044af9f00663bb3fa233d28c30e9a33e744c242448f414032948d`.

The law is fixed before the query. It uses sixteen tree prototypes and four cycle prototypes on each six-coordinate block, with three selected original columns. Its three branches have probabilities `1−2r, r, r`, with scales `1, 2, 1/r`, for `r = 2^(−ℓ)` and integer `ℓ ≥ 1`. In the declared metric `G_r = P + r²(I−P)`, the mean map is the identity and the risk is at most `5r` on unit queries. Here P is the K4 cut-space projector; repeated blocks use the block-diagonal metric from the source. The literal prototype table costs 1,440 bits, before format, interpreter, variable metadata, and runtime representation. The fair-bit bound is `ℓ+4` per block.

Test whether fewer columns become fewer physical bytes or lower latency at matched error. Include the current product control and the source's restricted fixed-diagonal comparator. “Nearly six versus three” is an asymptotic statement in the stipulated layout; it is not a claim about every Cassette sampler.

NM02 must check the zero-probability branch at `ℓ=1`, all scaled prototypes, exact mean, hard support, bit accounting, finite arithmetic, and the original-column page map. A categorical branch may map to three pages; it must also identify the full linear coefficient map. Keep this construction distinct from the later four-coordinate, query-selected two-rule sampler, which is outside the requested scope.
