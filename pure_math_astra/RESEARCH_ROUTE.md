# Route to the Cassette mathematics goal

The end state is a substantive original theorem that changes a consequential
Cassette capability or resource bound under assumptions the application can
satisfy, followed by a paper whose proof and contribution survive review.
The research has not reached that state.

## Current mathematical position

Two candidates have proved consequences in declared models.

| Candidate | What the proofs establish | What remains unestablished |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Query-selected sampling | A finite-word source family permits at most \(2m\) original-column reads at an error level for which every qualifying static nonlinear decoder with finite source advice needs more than \(2.02m\) expected reads on some source. | A feasible catalog and complete resource account that preserve a consequential advantage; a substantive original step beyond the closest prior work. |
| Full linear corrections | In the K4 construction, an explicit bounded-bit sampler uses \(3m\) original columns at an error level requiring nearly \(6m\) expected columns from diagonal corrections. | A consequential Cassette use for this restricted comparison, including the value of alternative permitted representations; substantive originality. |

The first statement is consolidated in
[the finite-word theorem](notes/finite_word_nonlinear_static_adaptive_separation.md).
The second is recorded in
[the K4 sampler](notes/k4_bounded_bit_full_linear_sampler.md) and
[its page account](notes/k4_dyadic_full_linear_page_accounting.md).
These are multiple mathematical results. They are not multiple demonstrated
improvements to Cassette.

## Active route and decisions

1. **Decide whether the sampling advantage survives its costs.** Construct
   or quantitatively bound the shared four-coordinate catalog, its selector,
   its coefficients, and its random-bit and arithmetic requirements. Charge
   those costs beside finite source words and the declared page layout.
   The next decision is whether a realizable parameter range retains a
   consequential resource advantage. Existence of a finite catalog alone
   does not answer that question.
   [The resource assessment](notes/finite_word_separation_resource_assessment.md)
   identifies the next concrete object: a serialized rational catalog with
   an exact selector and sampler, whose byte size and work can be counted.
2. **Resolve the obstruction exposed by that account.** If catalog size,
   precision, or the lower bound's dimension requirement prevents the
   application case, work on that named mathematical obstruction. An
   explicit compact sampler or a stronger finite-dimensional lower bound
   would advance this route. Additional lemmas that leave the obstruction
   unchanged do not advance this candidate's acceptance.
3. **Test the exact contribution against prior work.** Compare the surviving
   statement and its hardest proof step with the closest primary sources,
   including alternative terminology. State the intellectual advance
   precisely. A correct combination of standard tools earns no novelty
   claim merely because its notation or application differs.
4. **Commission the acceptance assessment.** Require separate findings on
   correctness, originality, and sufficient significance. The application
   consequence must follow with its declared costs and assumptions. Begin
   the paper only when all findings support the objective; review the
   completed paper and reopen the investigation if its claims fail.

The K4 candidate remains a second route, not a substitute acceptance
standard. Its next useful result would connect its explicit savings to a
Cassette-relevant operator class or settle the representation comparison.
The moving-projector geometry remains supporting work unless its next
result changes one of these consequential questions.

## Continuation rule

Each continuation should resolve a stated uncertainty, construct a needed
object, refute an approach, or strengthen the application or originality
case. Record the result and how it changes the next question in
[the ledger](RESEARCH_LEDGER.md). If a candidate proves routine or its
costs erase its proposed consequence, retain its proofs as supporting work
and change the research direction. Do not weaken the objective to call
that outcome success.

The stopping condition is the accepted theorem, application case, and
reviewed paper. Elapsed effort, the number of notes, user pressure, and a
finished draft do not establish that condition.
