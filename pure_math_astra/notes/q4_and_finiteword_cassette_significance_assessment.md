# q4_and_finiteword_cassette_significance_assessment.md — bounded Cassette application assessment of the q=4 lower bound and finite-word adaptive gap; depends on ../../MATHS.md, critical_scale_2x2_polar_lemma_proof.md, exact_graph_chart_polar_bridge_review.md, finite_word_growing_static_adaptive_separation.md, rademacher_source_growing_static_adaptive_review.md, k4_bounded_bit_full_linear_sampler.md.

## Verdict

The global real \(q=4\) result and the finite-word growing construction are
mathematically useful execution-layer results. Only the finite-word result
changes a fresh-read boundary that Cassette could adopt as a declared,
restricted execution class. Neither presently establishes a general Cassette
resource frontier. A comparison with all legal descriptions and page actions
would be one stronger route to such a frontier; it is not a prerequisite for
the restricted result to matter.

The \(K_4\) bounded-bit sampler is an exact, finite-randomness upper
construction in its own original-column model. Its diagonal comparator is
too narrow to fill that gap.

No originality conclusion is made in this assessment.

## 1. The exact execution bridge that is already available

Let an execution return

\[
\widehat y(x)=Bx+RZ(x),\qquad R=A-B,
\]

where \(\mathbb E Z(x)=x\) and each realized \(Z(x)\) has at most \(s\)
nonzero coordinates. If each nonzero coordinate causes the corresponding
original residual column to be fetched, then this is an exact hard-cap
coefficient execution rule. Its error identity is

\[
\mathbb E\|\widehat y(x)-Ax\|_2^2
=\mathbb E\,(Z(x)-x)^*R^*R(Z(x)-x). \tag{1}
\]

Thus the query-adaptive atomic quantity \(\Psi_s(R^*R)\) is exactly the
worst-unit-query mean-square error of this declared residual execution
class. This is not an analogy with the execution model in MATHS.md; it is
the displayed equality. It extends the coordinate sampler in MATHS.md
without changing its exact-mean requirement. It becomes a fresh-traffic
statement only after a source format specifies the bytes needed to fetch a
selected residual column, as MATHS.md requires (lines 390--492).

Equation (1) is also the boundary of the bridge. It does not cover a
resident reconstruction that changes after the query, an output map that is
not \(RZ(x)\), a nonlinear decoder acting on fetched content, or a page that
delivers more useful information than its named residual column.

## 2. Global real \(q=4\) basis lower bound

### Correctness

The finite polar lemma and graph-chart transfer establish

\[
 \Psi_2\bigl(P+r^2(I-P)\bigr)\ge r/360
 \quad(0<r\le1)
\tag{2}
\]

for every real rank-two orthogonal projector \(P\) on \(\mathbb R^4\).
The conclusion is uniform in the coordinate basis. A diagonal projector has
a two-coordinate exact-mean law with risk at most \(r\), so the order in
\(r\) is sharp within that coefficient model. The proof records no
unproved limiting passage; the transfer is finite-dimensional.

Via (1), a no-resident-description atom \(A=R\) with
\(A^*A=P+r^2(I-P)\) cannot achieve a two-column exact-unbiased execution
error below \(r/360\) on every unit query. In the particular relative
normalization of MATHS.md, \(\|A\|_F^2=2+2r^2\), so such a plan must have

\[
 \varepsilon_{\rm exec}^2\ge {r\over720(1+r^2)}. \tag{3}
\]

This is a valid conditional execution lower bound, not merely a statement
about coordinates.

### Cassette consequence and declared conditions

The result blocks one tempting inference: choosing a real basis for a
four-coordinate rank-two residual cannot make the hard-two-read,
query-adaptive error \(o(r)\). It would materially constrain a Cassette
atom only if the plan declares all of the following:

1. the residual is represented by its four original columns and has Gram
   matrix \(P+r^2(I-P)\), up to a certified scalar factor;
2. every correction has the form in (1) with at most two fetched columns;
3. the resident description is fixed before the query and is either
   \(B=0\), or is proved unable to move the residual outside this family;
   and
4. the source/page format charges each selected original column and records
   the resulting \(t_{\rm fresh}\).

No present theorem supplies item 3 for Cassette's full description class.
MATHS.md deliberately allows an arbitrary declared reconstruction \(B\),
and says that a distortion infimum alone does not name an executable
description (lines 437--489). It also keeps a physical page conversion,
operation-specific composition, and the protected trace contract separate
(lines 491--492 and 769--782). Therefore (2) is a strong inner-execution
obstruction, but not an impossibility theorem for a general Cassette atom.

### Originality and significance status

Originality is unassessed here. The theorem's present significance is
foundational: it prevents an unsupported basis-optimization escape from a
specified hard-cap residual model. A plan that actually declares that model
can use (2) as a material execution constraint. No current Cassette atom or
page contract has yet been shown to require that model.

## 3. Finite-word growing static/adaptive separation

### Correctness

For a fixed source-independent \(b\)-bit resident interpreter, the reviewed
Rademacher construction supplies a dyadic source \(A_m\) with
\(p=O_\eta(m+b)\). A shared finite query-selected block catalog uses at
most \(2m\) original columns and has worst-unit-query error at most

\[
 {4883\over2560}\eta.
\tag{4}
\]

Every comparator in the stipulated static class—one pre-query resident
matrix and a query-independent exact-mean row-sparse linear residual
operator, even after a free fixed unitary coordinate change—needs more than

\[
 (2+1/50)m
\tag{5}
\]

columns to meet that error. The codebook avoidance quantifiers are the
important part: the source is selected after the finite interpreter range
is fixed, while the encoder may still choose its \(b\)-bit state after
seeing the source. The construction permits no free source-trained decoder
state.

The two paths fit (1): the adaptive path takes \(B=0\) and a query-selected
coefficient law, while the static path has a fixed \(B\) and fixed
operator-unbiased law. The theorem therefore proves a growing hard-cap
fresh-column separation inside that declared execution class.

### Cassette consequence

This is the stronger application result. Cassette can choose an
original-column, column-linear execution class with a source-independent
interpreter and charge all learned decoder parameters in the resident word.
Under those assumptions, (4)--(5) prove that making the sampling law
query-dependent can save at least \(m/50\) fresh columns at the same
worst-query mean-square error. The source dimension can remain linear in
\(m+b\), so this is not an artifact of a quadratic lift.

That is a real restricted capability and resource boundary. It is not yet
a Cassette-wide frontier. The theorem does not compare against
query-selected reconstructions, nonlinear decoders, general encoded-page
operations, or outputs outside the selected-column span. Those operations
are permitted unless a plan declares otherwise.

### What remains for a real Cassette instance

The finite-word result is consequential within its declared class; it need
not first become a lower bound for every possible description or decoder.
To make it evidence for a Cassette plan, the remaining work is concrete:

1. name an atom/source family that the plan is permitted to use and whose
   stored layout realizes the construction;
2. give a finite encoding and byte count for the catalog, selector,
   random-bit procedure, interpreter, and residual-addressing records;
3. convert a selected column into actual page reads, bytes, memory, and
   latency for that layout; and
4. place the one-step error in an operation-specific composition and trace
   record if the plan claims more than one step.

Those are the execution-certificate fields required by MATHS.md. They are
not supplied by finite existence of a catalog or by an abstract support
count.

### A stronger, optional generalization

A column-page reduction would make a materially stronger statement:

> **Column-page reduction.** For a declared source family and page format,
> every legal pre-query resident execution using at most \(b\) description
> bits and \(s\) fresh page reads reduces to a decoded matrix \(B\) plus a
> query-independent exact-mean row-\(s\)-sparse linear coefficient law;
> each nonzero coefficient requires one charged page read.

It would compare the adaptive law with every static execution admitted by
that named format. This is not currently required to use the restricted
gap, and it must not be substituted for the concrete accounting items
above. Without it, the finite-word lower bound still has its stated force
against its declared static comparator, but no more.

### Originality and significance status

Originality is unassessed here. The finite-word result has conditional
application significance because it establishes a linear read gap in an
execution class Cassette can explicitly declare and account for. The
present evidence lacks an actual atom/source contract and the numerical
accounting needed to show that this class changes a Cassette plan's chosen
resource point. It has not passed a broader-frontier test, which is a
stronger and separate claim.

## 4. Why the bounded-bit \(K_4\) sampler does not close the gap

The \(K_4\) construction has an exact mean, at most three selected rows,
risk at most \(5r\), and a worst-case fair-bit cost \(\ell+4\) for
\(r=2^{-\ell}\). Its dense aligned-page model also makes its three selected
columns cost exactly \(3B_{\rm page}\) per block. These are useful facts:
they show that finite random-bit accounting need not be an obstacle for one
full-linear law.

They do not supply the column-page reduction above. The lower comparison
there is only against diagonal fixed laws, and its page statement depends
on a deliberately declared dense aligned layout. Its \(K_4\) Gram family
is also not the finite-word equicorrelation family. It therefore cannot be
used as evidence about arbitrary execution, arbitrary resident
reconstructions, or the growing theorem's static comparator.

## Overall disposition

Correctness: the two stated execution-layer results are supported in their
declared models. Originality: not assessed. Significance: the finite-word
gap is the only current result that changes a resource boundary Cassette can
adopt as a restricted class; the q=4 lower is a necessary internal
obstruction for a narrower residual model. No result here authorizes a
general Cassette frontier claim or acceptance of the larger mathematics
goal.
