# Research ledger

## Acceptance boundary

A lead result must satisfy all four conditions.

1. It states a new mathematical object or theorem inside Cassette's actual model.
2. A complete proof survives counterexample search and independent reconstruction.
3. It changes a certificate, bound, or feasible plan by more than a cosmetic reformulation.
4. A current primary-source search finds no prior statement of the same conjunction, or the paper
   narrows its novelty claim to the exact uncollided part.

## Loop 1 — minimal-nonface realization compression

Status: surviving lemma; rejected as the lead result.

Deleting balanced subset blocks from Theorem 3 reduces the square construction from
`n(2^(n-1)-1)` to the total incidence of the minimal nonfaces. The deduction is useful and exact,
but it is close to pruning the proof by its own obstruction definition. It does not yet meet the
requested level of foundational change.

## Loop 2 — observation-obstruction coloring

Status: proved candidate; held for collision pressure.

For observation support fibres, zero-error selection is equivalent to every fibre being a face.
The minimum state-only side alphabet is the weak chromatic number of the minimal nonfaces that
occur inside at least one fibre. This materially completes the present qualitative Blackwell
section, but it resembles classical zero-error source coding with decoder side information.

## Loop 3 — sequential residual witnesses

Status: proved application; rejected as the lead result.

Join-tree messages give an exact local-to-global certificate for finite causal constraint scopes.
Conditional hazards sharpen the union risk bound, and martingale differences replace independence
for quadrature. These are useful integrations of established mathematics, not yet a plausible new
theorem.

## Loop 4 — minimax residual-access variance

Status: lead theorem survived independent proof reconstructions, a fixed-seed finite counterexample
search that is not a proof, and the bounded primary-source collision search; written as a separate
mathematical paper.

For a declared decomposition of a residual operator into page-addressable components, minimize the
largest eigenvalue of the exact sampling covariance over all query-independent sampling laws. The
candidate has an exact dual, an exact sample-complexity statement inside the estimator class, and
closed forms that can be arbitrarily sharper than Frobenius-energy sampling.

The broad E-optimal inverse-probability problem is established prior art. The surviving contribution
is narrower: a global reciprocal-weight dual valid at repeated top eigenvalues, exact optimality
witnesses, the two-column and orthogonal closed forms, an exact criterion for when the existing
squared-column-norm law is minimax, and the complete rank-one geometry. No source in the recorded
three-wave search states that conjunction for the declared estimator class. A finite search cannot
establish literature-wide novelty.
